We could not be more grateful for all of the things that are unfurling this late in the season. Charlie loves to crawl around the broccoli patch and nibble from each one of the heads of broccoli that are growing quite nicely. Charlie is quite literally a Cabbage Patch baby because he will roll and roll all over the cabbages which are heading up perfectly.

This past weekend we put up a ton of kimchi which was amazing and all made from our cabbage.

NEWS! We’re going to be opening applications in December for our Kitchen Garden Cohort, where we will work with three to five families from diverse backgrounds over 2027! The goal is to help consult on homesteading or garden desires and needs. We also aim to help connect all families with resources to help with common barriers to thriving as homesteaders including time, financial burden and infrastructure. We’ll meet quarterly and offer site visits as well to help make a concrete, feasible and joyful kitchen garden strategy. Exciting!! Application cycle opens Dec 1st! We’ll post a link!

Abundance offerings? We have plenty of kale to share and look forward to sharing heads of cabbage soon which we can’t possibly get through all of them. Swing by the porch, or we can leave you a full cooler just text us what you’re looking for.

Charlie loves to crawl around the broccoli patch and nibble from each one of the heads of broccoli that are growing quite nicely. Charlie is quite literally a Cabbage Patch baby because he will roll and roll all over the cabbages which are heading up perfectly. This past weekend we put up a ton of kimchi which was amazing and all made from our cabbage. Abundance offerings? We have plenty of kale to share and look forward to sharing heads of cabbage which we can't possibly get through all of them soon. Please also reach out by text or email in the event that you're interested in joining a shop night Friday, which usually starts around 6 PM or if you're interested in a blacksmithing workshop which will be hosted one of the next two Saturdays.

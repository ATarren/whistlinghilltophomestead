A little about us

A little about
Whistling Hilltop

Whistling Hilltop Homestead serves as a hub in Easthampton, Massachusetts for tiny urban kitchen garden experiments. We value food sovereignty and community through the ongoing development of a sub-quarter-acre gardenscape. Our experimental kitchen garden ventures focus on the vital rhythms of native pollinators, growing wild corners full of fruit, and enjoying quiet moments spent fully present in our tiny sanctuary with friends and family. The knowledge we have gathered through homesteading lives within a collection of seasonal plantings and harvests we continue to refine each year. We view these simple practices, from sowing to harvest, as family heirlooms, grounding our commitment to traditional agricultural knowledge, nourishing local food systems, and, above all, building an inclusive, culture-rich community.

Every season brings something new. We’re taking it one day at a time and are happy to have you along for the journey.

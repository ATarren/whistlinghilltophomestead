Awards & Projects

A few milestones, shared efforts, and ideas taking root at Whistling Hilltop Homestead.

  • Mass Cultural Arts Grant

    The Mass Cultural Council awarded us a small grant to support our homesteading and community culture-building events and activities.

    Awarded · 2026

  • Hadley Chili Competition

    Charlie and Paul grew all the peppers and vegetables for their entry—and won by a landslide among fifteen entries from neighboring towns.

    Awarded · 2025

  • A Kitchen Garden Cohort

    We’re building a lawn-to-kitchen-garden homesteading cohort, partnering with three to five families to meet quarterly, share knowledge, and create small networks of support across our diverse community.

    Accepting applications · 2026–2027
